NAME
SANDWAVE - Cymatic Voice Plate (VST3, AU)

SUMMARY
Sing, speak or play into SANDWAVE and watch your sound draw sand patterns on a vibrating plate in real time, like a Chladni plate. 14 vibration modes, a square and a round plate, in a VST3 and AU plugin.

TAGS
visualizer, vst3, audio unit, cymatics, chladni, voice, microphone, ableton, plugin, music production

DESCRIPTION

SANDWAVE is a cymatics visualizer by Uranium Music. Put it on any audio track and it turns the sound into sand on a vibrating metal plate. The loudest frequency in the sound picks how the plate vibrates, the loudness shakes the sand, and the grains slide off the moving areas and gather on the still lines, drawing the figures known from Chladni plates. Your audio passes through untouched.

YOUR VOICE, DRAWN IN SAND
- Works with a microphone, a vocal, an instrument or a full mix
- Finds the loudest frequency from 40 Hz to 5 kHz in real time and shows it on the display
- Low sounds draw simple figures, high sounds draw dense ones
- When the sound stops, the sand stays where it settled, like on a real plate
- The level meter and the light at the top show how hard the plate is ringing

CONTROLS
- GAIN: input level before the analysis, -30 to +30 dB
- SENS: how quiet a sound still moves the sand
- GRAIN: how far the grains jump
- DECAY: how long the plate keeps ringing after the sound stops
- RANGE: how wide a frequency range the 14 modes cover
- FREQ: pick a mode by hand; with MIC IN off the plate rings on its own, no input needed
- MIC IN, SQUARE, CIRCLE and SHAKE buttons

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
- macOS 11 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel): VST3 and AU
- Windows 10 or later (64-bit): VST3
- A DAW that loads VST3 or AU effects, such as Ableton Live

GOOD TO KNOW
- SANDWAVE is a visualizer: it does not change your sound.
- Microphone: put SANDWAVE on an audio track whose input is your microphone and turn on monitoring (or arm the track) so the sound reaches the plugin.
- Activation: enter your e-mail address and license key the first time you open the plugin. An internet connection is required.
- Online check: the license is checked each time the plugin loads. Without internet, it keeps working for 72 hours after the last successful check.
- macOS installer: if macOS says it can't verify the installer, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
- Windows installer: if SmartScreen appears, click More info > Run anyway.
