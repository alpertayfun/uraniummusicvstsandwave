YOUR LICENSE KEY

Your license key (URAN-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) is sent to the e-mail address you used for this purchase. Check your spam folder if you can't find it.

INSTALL ON macOS

1. Open the .dmg and double-click "SANDWAVE Installer.pkg".

2. If macOS says it can't verify the installer, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

3. Follow the installer steps.

INSTALL ON WINDOWS

1. Run the Setup file.

2. If SmartScreen appears, click More info > Run anyway.

3. Follow the installer steps.

ACTIVATE

1. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins. In Ableton Live, go to Plug-Ins in Settings (Preferences on Windows), turn on "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders" (and "Use Audio Units" on Mac), then click Rescan.

2. Load "SANDWAVE" from Uranium Music on an audio track.

3. Enter your e-mail address and license key, then click ACTIVATE. An internet connection is required.

START DRAWING

Put SANDWAVE on a vocal, an instrument or a whole mix and press play. To use your voice live, set the audio track's input to your microphone and turn on monitoring (or arm the track), then sing or speak. With MIC IN off, turn FREQ to make the plate ring on its own. Switch between the square and the round plate, and press SHAKE to pour fresh sand.

GOOD TO KNOW

The license is checked each time the plugin loads. Without internet, it keeps working for 72 hours after the last successful check.
