SANDWAVE by Uranium Music
=========================

This installs the SANDWAVE VST3 plugin to the standard VST3 folder:

    C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\SANDWAVE.vst3

After installing:

1. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins.
   Ableton Live: Options -> Preferences -> Plug-Ins -> enable
   "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders" -> Rescan.

2. Load "SANDWAVE" (Uranium Music) on an audio track (a microphone input works) and enter your
   e-mail address and license key when asked.

You can uninstall SANDWAVE from Settings -> Apps.
