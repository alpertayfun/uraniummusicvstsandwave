SANDWAVE by Uranium Music
=========================

1. Double-click "SANDWAVE Installer.pkg" and follow the steps.
   If macOS says the installer is from an unidentified developer:
   right-click (or Control-click) the installer, choose "Open", then "Open" again.

2. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins.
   Ableton Live: Settings -> Plug-Ins -> enable "Use Audio Units" and
   "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders" -> Rescan.

3. Load "SANDWAVE" (Uranium Music) on an audio track (a microphone input works) and enter your
   e-mail address and license key when asked.

Installed files:
  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/SANDWAVE.vst3
  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/SANDWAVE.component

To uninstall, delete those two files.
