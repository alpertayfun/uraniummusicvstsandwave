SANDWAVE

A cymatic voice plate visualizer by Uranium Music.

This installs:

  • VST3 → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/SANDWAVE.vst3
  • Audio Unit → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/SANDWAVE.component

You will need your license key and the e-mail address it was issued to the first time you open the plugin.
